🔹 **Slide 2: What problem are we trying to solve?**

**Problem description:**
Manual tracking of solar maintenance is inefficient, leading to missed services, system downtime, and lack of transparency between customers and technicians.

**Who is facing the problem?**
Solar Homeowners, AMC Providers, and Field Technicians.

**If interviews were conducted:**
Technicians reported difficulty in scheduling visits and tracking service history manually.

**Suggested Image:**
A split screen showing a messy innovative paper logbook vs. a clean digital dashboard.

🔹 **Slide 3: Name of Solution**

**Solution Name:** SolarCare
**Tagline:** Smart Solar Maintenance Management.

**Detailed explanation of solution:**
SolarCare is a comprehensive digital platform designed to streamline solar asset management. It connects customers with technicians for seamless maintenance scheduling, real-time ticket tracking, and automated service reminders, ensuring optimal system performance.

**Tools/technologies used:**
*   **Frontend:** React, Vite, Tailwind CSS
*   **Mobile:** Capacitor (Android/iOS)
*   **Backend:** Supabase (Auth, Database, Storage)
*   **Features:** Lucide React Icons, i18next (Internationalization)

**Why this approach is the best:**
The solution uses a cross-platform approach (Web & Mobile) ensures accessibility for all users. The integration of vernacular support and voice-enabled ticketing specifically addresses the needs of non-technical users in diverse regions.

🔹 **Slide 4: Prototype Demo**

**Key features:**
*   **AI AMC Recommendations:** Intelligence engine that suggests the perfect maintenance plan based on system age & capacity.
*   **Voice & Photo Ticketing:** Report complex issues instantly using voice notes and images—no typing needed.
*   **Multilingual Support:** Full app available in English, Hindi, Marathi, and Kannada for rural accessibility.
*   **emergency Quick-Dial:** One-tap SOS button to instantly connect with support teams during breakdowns.
*   **Role-Based Dashboards:** Dedicated, secure interfaces for Customers, Technicians, and Admins.
*   **Digital Warranty Vault:** Tracks warranty expiry for Panels, Inverters, and Structure in one place.
*   **Offline-Ready Android App:** Built with Capacitor for seamless performance standard low-end devices.
*   **Automated Scheduling:** Smart slot booking for site visits and health checks.

**How each feature solves the problem:**
*   **AMC Tracking:** Eliminates missed maintenance visits and ensures system longevity.
*   **Voice Tickets:** Reduces friction for users to report complex technical issues.
*   **Vernacular Support:** Makes the technology accessible to rural and semi-urban populations.
*   **Dashboards:** Provides transparency and accountability in service delivery.

**One strong highlight one-liner:**
"Empowering solar owners with proactive care and effortless support."

🔹 **Slide 5: Social Impact**

**Who benefits from the solution?**
*   **Rural Solar Owners:** Improved access to maintenance services in their local language.
*   **Local Technicians:** Organized workflow and consistent income through tracked AMCs.

**Research methods used:**
*   **Feature-Driven Development:** Identified need for non-textual interaction based on target user demographics.
*   **Competitor Analysis:** Evaluated existing gaps in post-sales service for distributed solar assets.

**Key findings:**
*   Language barriers often prevent effective communication of technical faults.
*   Voice notes provide richer context for technicians compared to text descriptions.

---

🔹 **Slide 6: Impact & Next Steps**

**What changes if scaled?**
Standardization of solar maintenance quality across regions, leading to higher energy generation efficiency and prolonged asset life.

**What happens if adopted by 1,000 users or across a city/district?**
We build a valuable data repository of system performance and common failure points, enabling predictive maintenance algorithms.

**Our Aspiration for this Project:**
To become the operating system for decentralized solar maintenance in emerging markets.

**Clear next steps after makeathon:**
1.  **Mobile Deployment:** Build and release the Android `.apk` via Capacitor.
2.  **Field Pilot:** Onboard 50 local solar installations for beta testing.
3.  **Partnership:** Collaborate with local solar installers to bundle SolarCare with new installations.

**Testing plan:**
*   **User Acceptance Testing (UAT):** Conduct sessions with technicians to validate the ticket closure workflow.
*   **Performance Testing:** Stress test the database with simulated high-volume ticket creation.

**Prototype improvement plan:**
*   Integrate IoT data for real-time generation monitoring.
*   Implement AI-based image analysis for health check photos.
